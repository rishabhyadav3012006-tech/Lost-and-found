How ReUnite AI Works

Three simple steps to help bring families together

1. Submit a Report

Fill in descriptive details about the missing or found person — appearance, location, clothing, and optional photos.

2. AI Matching

Our matching engine compares physical descriptors across all active reports and scores potential matches for human review.

3. Safe Contact

When a strong match is found, both parties are notified. Contact happens through our platform to protect everyone's privacy.
